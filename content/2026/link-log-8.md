Honestly, I've tried understanding the systemd hate, but it really doesn't make any sense. One of the main guys behind systemd [refuted a bunch of the criticisms](https://0pointer.de/blog/projects/the-biggest-myths) people have for it. It's also kind of insane how people's biggest gripe is that it doesn't follow UNIX philosophy while literally using an operating system called GNU's NOT UNIX!!! More than that, if you really don't like all the other stuff that systemd has that isn't the init system, you can literally just not have them on your machine because systemd is a modular program with over 60 binaries. Most distros do this already and don't use a lot of those binaries out of the box.
