[Roger Radio](https://theholyroger.com/Roger-Radio/) is an internet radio station where their crypto, The Holy Roger, determines what gets air time. If you have a song you want them to play, get your hands on some Roger and spin some tracks. Fun crypto deep dive with memes, good tunes, and an interesting take on how currency shapes taste and behavior.
